ABOUT FRESHTRACK AI

Smarter Logistics. Fresher Food.

FreshTrack AI is an AI-powered cold-chain intelligence platform designed to help monitor food shipments, understand freshness and identify spoilage risks.

🌡️

Monitor

Monitor important shipment conditions such as temperature and humidity.

🧠

Analyze

Analyze shipment data to understand the condition of food during transportation.

📊

Predict

Use AI-based insights to estimate freshness and spoilage risk.

⚠️

Act

Receive alerts and recommendations so that action can be taken at the right time.

Our Goal

To reduce food waste and improve cold-chain visibility by providing simple and actionable intelligence.
